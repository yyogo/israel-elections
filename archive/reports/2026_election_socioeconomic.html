Method and limits

Settlement results were joined to LMS by numeric settlement code. All votes from a settlement inherit its single 2021 cluster; this describes places where votes were counted, not voters’ individual socioeconomic status. Within large cities, neighborhoods may differ substantially. No statistical-area or voter-residence data were supplied.

Unassigned settlements are unmatched by settlement code. External envelopes (449,357 valid votes) are kept separate because the source aggregates them under code 9999 rather than a settlement. Other lists includes all lists that did not win seats, preserving the full valid-vote denominator. The orange color family reflects Netanyahu’s election bloc; the other elected lists and Arab lists are visual groupings, not assertions of a unified bloc.

Source: supplied ballot-box file, reconciled to 5,690,682 valid votes; supplied LMS 2021 file. The supplied election totals match the published count as of 30 October 2026, 00:58 Israel time; formal certification may follow. See the accompanying CSVs for full numerical results and unmatched settlement codes.
