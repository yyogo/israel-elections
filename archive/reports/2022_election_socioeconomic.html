Method and limits

Settlement results were joined to LMS by numeric settlement code. All votes from a settlement inherit its single 2021 cluster; this describes places where votes were counted, not voters’ individual socioeconomic status. Within large cities, neighborhoods may differ substantially. No statistical-area or voter-residence data were supplied.

Unassigned settlements are unmatched by settlement code. External envelopes (458,714 valid votes) are kept separate because the source aggregates them under code 9999 rather than a settlement. Other lists includes all lists that did not win seats, preserving the full valid-vote denominator. The coalition color family reflects the coalition formed after the 2022 election; the other elected lists and Arab lists are visual groupings, not assertions of a unified bloc.

Source: supplied settlement and ballot-box files, reconciled to 4,764,742 valid votes; supplied LMS 2021 file. The supplied election totals match the Central Elections Committee’s 2022 results, rather than the 2026 election shown in the reference image. See the accompanying CSVs for full numerical results and unmatched settlement codes.
